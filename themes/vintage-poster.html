✦ The Process ✦

From chaos to clarity

Three acts. Zero configuration.

Act I

Schedule

Drop a link. Meet handles the rest — finds available slots, sends invites, and builds the agenda from your project context.

  • Calendar sync across all platforms
  • AI agenda generation from PRDs and tickets
  • Timezone-aware scheduling
🗓 Schedule
Act II

Run

During the meeting, Meet transcribes in real-time, tracks decisions, and flags when discussions drift off-agenda.

  • Live transcription with speaker ID
  • Off-topic detection and gentle course correction
  • Decision logging with confidence scores
🎙 Conduct
Act III

Follow-up

After the meeting, Meet generates a summary, extracts action items, and pushes them to your project management tools.

  • Auto-generated meeting notes
  • Action items assigned with deadlines
  • Sync to Jira, Linear, Notion, Slack
📝 Resolve
